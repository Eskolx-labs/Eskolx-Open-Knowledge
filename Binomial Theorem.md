The **Binomial Theorem** provides a formula for expanding powers of binomial expressions without repeated multiplication.

$(a+b)^n=∑(n,k) a^(n-k )b^k$

The coefficients

        (n,k)

are called **binomial coefficients** and correspond to entries in Pascal's Triangle.

Example:

$(a+b)^3=a3+3a2b+3ab2+b3(a+b)^3$ 

The theorem plays an important role in combinatorics, probability, algebra, and statistics.
