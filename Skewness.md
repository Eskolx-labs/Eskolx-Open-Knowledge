**Skewness** measures the asymmetry of a distribution around its mean. It helps determine whether a dataset leans more heavily toward smaller or larger values.

- **Positive skewness:** Long right tail, mean > median.
- **Negative skewness:** Long left tail, mean < median.
- **Zero skewness:** Symmetric distribution.
Skewness=$E(X−μ)3 / σ3$
Skewness is useful for identifying departures from normality and understanding how data are distributed.
