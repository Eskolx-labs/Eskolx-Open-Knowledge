The **mean**, commonly known as the average, is one of the most widely used measures of central tendency in statistics. It represents the center or typical value of a dataset by summing all observations and dividing by the total number of observations. The mean is useful because it incorporates every data point in the dataset, making it sensitive to changes in individual values. However, extreme values (outliers) can significantly affect the mean, making it less representative for highly skewed data.
