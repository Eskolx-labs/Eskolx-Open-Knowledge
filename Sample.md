## Definition

A sample is a subset of a population selected for observation, measurement, or analysis.

## Intuition

Studying an entire population is often expensive or impractical. Instead, researchers study a smaller group and use it to learn about the whole population.

## Why It Matters

Most statistical analyses are performed on samples. The quality of conclusions depends on how well the sample represents the population.

## Example

Population: All university students in Ethiopia.

Sample: 1,000 students selected from universities across Ethiopia.
