# What is the difference between population and sample

## Definition
 **Population** is a collection of objects possessing the same characteristics that can be studied.
**Sample** is a small portion of the population
## Intuition
A population represents the whole group, while a sample represents a smaller part used to understand that group.
## Why it matters
Sampling saves time and resources while allowing us to draw conclusions about populations. but studying the whole population might be time, resource consuming and exhaustive work.
## How it works
We take samples from the whole group  to draw conclusions about the population.
## Example
a researcher wants to study the employment satisfaction in a company called Eskolx that has 1,000 employees and he selected  45 employees from them and conducted the study.
- Population = 1,000 employees
- Sample =45 employees
## Common Mistakes
- Assuming every sample represents the population accurately.
- Ignoring sampling bias.
## Implementation
Researchers use sampling methods and statistical techniques to estimate population characteristics from sample data.
