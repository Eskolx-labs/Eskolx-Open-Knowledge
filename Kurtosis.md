**Kurtosis** measures the heaviness of a distribution's tails and its propensity to produce extreme values. Contrary to common belief, kurtosis is more about tail behavior than peak height.

$Kurtosis=E(X−μ)^4 / σ4$

Types of kurtosis:

- **Mesokurtic:** Same kurtosis as a normal distribution.
- **Leptokurtic:** Heavier tails, more outliers.
- **Platykurtic:** Lighter tails, fewer outliers.

Kurtosis helps analysts assess the likelihood of rare or extreme events.
