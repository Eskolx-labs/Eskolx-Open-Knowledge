## Definition

A confidence interval is a range of values, computed from sample data, that is likely to contain an unknown population parameter.

## Intuition

A sample provides an estimate, but estimates are uncertain. A confidence interval expresses that uncertainty by providing a plausible range of values.

## Why It Matters

Confidence intervals provide more information than a single estimate by indicating the precision and reliability of the estimate.

## Example

A survey estimates the average height of students to be 170 cm.

95% Confidence Interval: 167 cm to 173 cm.

This suggests the true population mean is likely to lie within that range.

## Common Mistakes

- Interpreting a confidence interval as a guarantee.
- Assuming a wider interval is always better.
- Ignoring the impact of sample size on interval width.
