## Definition

The probability distribution of a statistic computed from many possible samples of the same size.

## Intuition

If we repeatedly draw samples and calculate a statistic (such as the mean), those statistics form a distribution.

## Example

Taking many random samples of 100 students and computing the average age for each sample.

## Why It Matters

Sampling distributions are the foundation of confidence intervals and statistical inference.
