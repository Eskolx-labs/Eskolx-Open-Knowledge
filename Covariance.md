**Covariance** measures the joint variability of two random variables. It indicates whether variables tend to move together or in opposite directions. Positive covariance means both variables increase or decrease together, while negative covariance means one tends to increase when the other decreases. A covariance close to zero suggests little or no linear relationship.

$Cov(X,Y)=E[(X−μX)(Y−μY)]$

Unlike correlation, covariance is not standardized, making its magnitude difficult to interpret directly.

**Applications:** Portfolio optimization, multivariate statistics, regression analysis, and feature engineering.
