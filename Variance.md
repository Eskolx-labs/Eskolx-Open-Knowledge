**Variance** measures the degree to which data points differ from the mean. It quantifies the spread or dispersion of a distribution by calculating the average squared deviation from the mean. A low variance indicates that most values are clustered around the mean, while a high variance suggests that observations are more widely scattered. Because deviations are squared, variance is always non-negative and gives more weight to larger deviations.
