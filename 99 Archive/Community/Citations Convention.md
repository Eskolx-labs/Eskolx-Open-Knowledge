# Citations Convention

How we cite sources in Eskolx notes. The preferred pattern is a **link to a summary note with an embedded source**. Plain citations are the fallback.

## Priority

1. **Preferred:** a `[[summary note]]` that embeds the source itself:
   - Free paper → a summary note with the paper or web clipping embedded inside it
   - Web clipping → the clipping itself embedded inside the summary note
   - The summary note lives in the right folder for its type; the embed brings the content to the reader.
2. **Fallback:** a plain citation line with author, year, title, venue, URL. Used when the source isn't free, can't be clipped, or isn't worth a note yet.

## Where it applies

- **Research notes** (always). The References section of every research note.
- **Concept notes**, when a definition or claim comes from a source.
- **Tutorials**, when teaching material references a paper or docs.

## Why

- Citations that link somewhere inside the vault are discoverable and survive; bare URLs rot.
- Embedding keeps the knowledge usable even if the source disappears.
- A plain citation is fine. It's the fallback, not a failure. Just don't silently drop provenance.
