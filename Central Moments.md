**Central moments** are statistical quantities calculated relative to the mean of a distribution. They provide detailed information about the distribution's shape. The k-th central moment is defined as the expected value of the k-th power of deviations from the mean.
$μk = E(X−μ)^k$
Important central moments:

- First central moment = 0
- Second central moment = Variance
- Third central moment = Basis of Skewness
- Fourth central moment = Basis of Kurtosis

Central moments are fundamental tools for characterizing probability distributions.
