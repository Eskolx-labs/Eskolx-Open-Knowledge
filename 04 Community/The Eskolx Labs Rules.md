# The Eskolx Labs Rules

Keep these in both vaults.

## Rule 1

**If it matters, write it down.**

## Rule 2

**If it is private, keep it in Core.**

## Rule 3

**If it is meant to teach, make it Open.**

## Rule 4

**One note, one job.**

**Folders are broad domains; links and properties carry meaning.**

## Rule 6

**Properties exist because we query them.**

**No plugin without a real problem.**

## Rule 11

**No secrets in Markdown.**

## Rule 12

**Public knowledge must stand on its own.**

## Rule 13

**The vault is not the project.**

## Rule 14

**Optimize observed friction, not imaginary friction.**

## Rule 15

**The system must remain portable without Obsidian.**
